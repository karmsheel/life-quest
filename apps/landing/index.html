01 The premise

Your life is not a dataset

Your life is the last thing you should rent.

Most tools keep your plans, notes and decisions on someone else's server, then call the account a feature.

LifeQuest is the first fully private personal agentic harness: a local-first studio where agents work from your own context, on your own machine. You open the folder. You own what is in it.

Four domains / Five wings / One vault

  1. 01

    Own the file.

    Your vault is a folder of plain files. Copy it, back it up, open it offline, leave with it. No hosted replica sits behind a login.

  2. 02

    Own the context.

    Notes, memories, decisions and sources live on your machine, not ours. Agents read what you choose to put in front of them.

  3. 03

    Own the call.

    Agents propose. You approve. Every governed write waits in one inbox, and every change records who made it.

A life should not require a login to exist.
